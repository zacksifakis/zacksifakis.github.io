About

I'm a fourth-year PhD student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Dimitris Papailiopoulos. My research interests are LLM reasoning, test-time compute and verification, evaluation of language models, and LLM agents.

Before moving to Madison, I completed my BSc and integrated MEng (Diploma) in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Technical University of Crete. There, I worked with Prof. Aggelos Bletsas on asynchronous inference algorithms with a focus on security applications.

I am looking for research and ML internships for summer 2027.

Research

Selecting among sampled LLM reasoning traces

Lightweight hidden-state probes recover 34–40% of the headroom over majority voting on AIME across three model families (Qwen3, LFM2.5, DeepSeek-R1-Distill). The work separates coverage from selection and proposes a vote-weighting diagnostic.

Manuscript in preparation

In-context learning in superposition

Pretrained LLMs can execute multiple tasks at once from mixed-task prompts. Experiments on GPT-3.5, Llama-3 70B and Qwen-1.5 72B.
